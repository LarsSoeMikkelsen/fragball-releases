What's in it

Four modes

Capture the flag, team deathmatch, free for all and king of the hill, up to 4 against 4.

Four weapons, always

A hitscan rifle, a lightning beam, rockets and lobbed grenades. Everyone carries all four, plus a boost to dash and ram.

Abilities

Pick one before the match: heal field, shield wall, guard or overcharge field. Rare, but they turn a fight. Use it with E.

Bots that play like people

Line them up in the lobby and give each its own skill, so a newer player can have strong teammates against easier bots.

Maps with their own rules

Lava burns up close, frozen lakes are slippery, water blocks the way. Build your own maps in the editor.

Online and LAN

Host online and your game is listed for everyone, with a code to share. No ports to open. Or play on your LAN.

Getting started

Windows

Unzip and run fragball.exe. The game isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info → Run anyway.

Linux

Unzip and run ./fragball.x86_64.

Playing together

Everyone needs the same version. Online → Host online lists your game for everyone and gives you a 4-letter code to share. On the same network, use LAN.
